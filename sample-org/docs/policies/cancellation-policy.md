# Brightmere Goods: Order Cancellation Policy

*Customer Service policy CS-05. Owner: Head of Customer Service. Last reviewed March 2024.*

## 1. When an order can be cancelled

An order can be cancelled until it has been delivered. Once it is delivered, customers use the refund policy instead.

## 2. Who can cancel

Customer service agents may cancel orders placed in the **last 24 hours**. Older orders need a supervisor's approval, because the warehouse may already have picked them.

## 3. What happens

When an order is cancelled, the shipment is stopped and the customer gets a cancellation email.
