# Brightmere Goods: Refund Policy

*Customer Service policy CS-04. Owner: Head of Customer Service. Last reviewed March 2024.*

## 1. Refund window

Customers may request a refund within **30 days of delivery**. This applies to all customers, whether they shop online or in store.

## 2. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Partial refunds may be given at the agent's discretion, for example when one item in an order is damaged.

## 3. Approvals

Customer service agents may approve refunds up to **$500**. Larger refunds need a supervisor's approval.

## 4. Customers in the European Union

Customers in the EU have a legal **right to withdraw from a distance purchase within 14 days** of delivery, without giving a reason. A refund must be issued within 14 days of the customer telling us they are withdrawing. This right applies in addition to section 1 and cannot be refused because of section 3.

## 5. Gift cards

Orders paid with a gift card are refunded to a new gift card.
