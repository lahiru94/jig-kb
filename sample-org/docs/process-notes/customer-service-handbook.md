# Customer Service: Team Notes

*Internal notes kept by the customer service team. Not an official policy document.*

## Handling refund requests

1. Check the order has been delivered.
2. Check it is inside the refund window. The system will tell you if it isn't.
3. For damaged items, a partial refund for just that item is fine.
4. Over $500? It goes to the supervisor queue automatically. Supervisors approve almost all of these, so tell the customer it will be sorted within a day.

## Gift card refunds (manual)

The system cannot refund to a gift card properly, so we do this by hand:

1. Issue the refund in the system as normal.
2. Add a row to the **"GC refunds" spreadsheet** on the shared drive: order number, amount, date.
3. Every Friday, Priya in Finance reconciles the spreadsheet against the gift card provider's statement and corrects the ledger.

If Priya is away, the reconciliation waits until she is back.

## Gold members

Gold members sometimes say they were told they have longer to return things. If the system accepts the refund, go ahead.
