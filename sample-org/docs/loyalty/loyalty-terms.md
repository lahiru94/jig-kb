# Brightmere Rewards: Programme Terms

*Marketing. Version 3, January 2025.*

## Earning points

- **Standard members** earn 1 point per $1 spent, before tax.
- **Gold members** earn 2 points per $1 spent.

Points are added when an order is placed.

## Tiers

Customers who spend more than $2,000 in a calendar year become Gold members for the following year.

Gold benefits:
- double points
- free standard shipping
- early access to sales

## Using points

100 points can be redeemed for $1 off a future order. Points expire 24 months after they are earned.

## Changes

We may change these terms with 30 days' notice to members.
